# Printer Maintenance Manual

**A practical guide to reliable, efficient, and long-lasting office printers.**

---

## Welcome

This manual sets out standard procedures for the daily operation, maintenance, and troubleshooting of office printers. Follow these guidelines consistently to reduce downtime, extend hardware life, improve print quality, and lower total cost of ownership. Review this manual whenever a device is added or replaced, and keep a printed copy near shared printers so operators can consult it without network access.

!!! info "Audience"
    This manual is written for IT support staff, facility managers, and designated printer operators. End users can also consult the **Paper Handling** and **Troubleshooting** sections for common problems. Operators should complete the first-time setup checklist in **Getting Started** before taking ownership of a device.

## Key Principles

1. **Preventive over reactive** — scheduled maintenance costs far less than emergency repairs.
2. **Clean hands, clean machine** — switch off and unplug the printer before cleaning internal parts.
3. **Use approved supplies** — third-party toner and paper can damage the device and void its warranty.
4. **Log every action** — record each task, the meter reading, and any parts replaced in the service log so you can spot trends over time.
5. **When in doubt, escalate** — never disassemble components that this manual does not cover.
